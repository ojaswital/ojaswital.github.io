I build computational pathology models for pancreatic cancer. My work turns H&E whole-slide images into measurements of the tumor microenvironment and tests whether they predict how patients do.

About

I'm a research engineer in Dr. Pallavi Tiwari's Integrated Diagnostics and Analytics (IDIA) Lab in the Department of Radiology at the University of Wisconsin–Madison. I work on pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC), with oncologists and pathologists, on problems where a better model could change a treatment decision.

Before this I spent four years at AIQ Solutions, where I helped build an FDA 510(k)-cleared PET/CT product and led survival analysis research. I care most about translational work: methods that hold up on external data and reach patients.

Research

  • Tumor microenvironment phenotypes in PDAC

    I classify tissue tiles into tumor, stroma, inflammatory and other compartments, embed each with pathology foundation models, and feed those phenotype-aware embeddings into survival models. The models are validated on an external cohort from PanCAN's SPARK platform.

    whole-slide imagingattention MILsurvival analysis

  • Treatment response and molecular subtype

    The same phenotyping pipeline supports two other questions: predicting molecular subtype from H&E, and predicting response to neoadjuvant therapy from pre-treatment biopsies and paired resections.

    neoadjuvant therapymolecular subtyping

  • Spatial graphs and multimodal data

    I'm building graph models of tissue where edges encode which compartments touch, and working toward combining pathology with radiology and clinical reports.

    graph neural networksradiology + pathologyLLMs for reports
